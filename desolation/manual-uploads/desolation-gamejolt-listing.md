The world fell apart. What's left of it hides after dark.

Desolation: Tears of the Moon is a free, browser-based survival-horror game — no install, just play. Scavenge and build up a base, assign your survivors to keep the camp running (farming, security, crafting), and manage what's left of a broken world by day. When night falls, the moon brings something else out with it.

- Base-building and survivor management — assign roles, keep everyone fed and safe
- Real day/night tension — the threats change once the sun goes down
- Atmospheric horror, not jump-scare horror — dread you can feel building
- Play free, right in your browser, at https://desolation.rastacamp.com
